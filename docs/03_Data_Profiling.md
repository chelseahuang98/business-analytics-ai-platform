## Initial Findings

### Database Structure

- The dataset consists of 9 relational tables.
- Orders is the central fact table linking customers, payments, reviews, and order items.
- Products and sellers provide product and merchant dimensions.

### Data Quality

- Most tables contain unique primary keys with no duplicate records.
- Missing values exist primarily in delivery timestamps, product information, and customer reviews.
- Overall data quality is suitable for business analytics after standard preprocessing.

### Business Potential

The dataset supports analyses in:

- Customer Segmentation
- Sales Performance
- Product Analytics
- Seller Performance
- Customer Satisfaction
- Delivery Performance
