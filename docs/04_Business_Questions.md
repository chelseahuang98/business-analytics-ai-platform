# Business Questions

## Objective

The objective of this phase is to define the key business questions that will guide the SQL analysis, Python analysis, Power BI dashboard, and AI-powered business insights assistant.

---

## Executive Performance

1. How have order volume, revenue, and average order value changed over time?

2. Which product categories and geographic regions contribute the most revenue?

3. What percentage of orders are delivered, cancelled, unavailable, or still in progress?

---

## Customer Analytics

4. Which customers generate the highest total revenue and order frequency?

5. What proportion of customers make repeat purchases?

6. How do customer purchasing patterns vary by city and state?

---

## Product and Seller Performance

7. Which product categories generate the highest sales volume and revenue?

8. Which sellers generate the most revenue, and which sellers have the highest order volume?

9. Which products or sellers consistently receive low customer review scores?

---

## Operations and Customer Experience

10. How often are orders delivered later than the estimated delivery date?

11. Is late delivery associated with lower customer review scores?

12. Which states or sellers experience the longest delivery times?

---

## Payment Analysis

13. Which payment methods are used most frequently?

14. How does payment behavior vary by order value and installment count?

15. Are higher-value orders more likely to use multiple installments?

---

## Initial Analysis Priorities

The first phase of analysis will focus on the following five questions:

1. How have revenue and order volume changed over time?
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
3. What proportion of customers make repeat purchases?
4. Is late delivery associated with lower review scores?
5. Which sellers generate the highest revenue and customer satisfaction?
