# Business Requirement

## Project Overview

This project simulates a real-world business analytics initiative for an e-commerce company.

The objective is to build an end-to-end analytics solution that helps business stakeholders monitor sales performance, understand customer behavior, and generate actionable insights using SQL, Python, Power BI, and Generative AI.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across products, customers, and regions.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and identify high-value customers.
- Evaluate seller and product performance.
- Monitor customer satisfaction through review analysis.
- Enable business users to explore insights using an AI-powered assistant.

---

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Business Needs |
|-------------|----------------|
| Executive Team | Monitor overall business performance and revenue trends |
| Marketing Team | Identify valuable customer segments and purchasing patterns |
| Sales Team | Track product and seller performance |
| Customer Experience Team | Monitor customer reviews and satisfaction |

---

## Data Sources

The project uses the publicly available **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset**, which contains customer, order, payment, product, seller, review, and geographic information.

---

## Expected Deliverables

- SQL business analysis
- Python exploratory data analysis
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- AI-powered business insights assistant
- Executive business recommendations
