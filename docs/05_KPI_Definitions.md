# KPI Definitions

## Total Revenue

Definition:
Sum of payment_value.

Business Meaning:
Represents the total amount paid by customers.

---

## Total Orders

Definition:
Count of distinct order_id.

Business Meaning:
Number of completed customer orders.

---

## Total Customers

Definition:
Count of distinct customer_id.

Business Meaning:
Unique customers who placed orders.

---

## Average Order Value (AOV)

Definition:
Total Revenue / Total Orders

Business Meaning:
Average spending per order.

---

## Average Items per Order

Definition:
Total Order Items / Total Orders

Business Meaning:
Average number of products purchased in each order.
